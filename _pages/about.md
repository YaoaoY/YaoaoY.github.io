I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the School of Life Sciences, Tsinghua University, where I began a direct-entry doctoral program in August 2026. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology in June 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and biology. I am interested in developing advanced AI methods to address challenging biological questions, with a current focus on scientific machine learning and structural biology.

I enjoy learning across disciplines and turning computational ideas into tools that can support biological discovery.

My current research interests include **AI for biology**, **scientific machine learning**, **structural biology**, and **cryo-EM model refinement**.
